# 🏥 InsuranceTech Mobile Experience – Business Analyst Case Study

## Overview
This case study reimagines the corporate health insurance mobile experience by introducing a dedicated corporate journey within an existing retail insurance application. The proposed solution improves employee access to corporate health benefits while enabling HR teams to manage employee insurance programs through secure integration with HRMS.

---

## Business Problem
Employees enrolled under corporate health insurance often struggle to distinguish between retail and employer-sponsored benefits, leading to confusion, increased HR dependency, and poor digital adoption.

---

## Proposed Solution
This case study proposes a feature-flag based corporate experience that introduces:

- Corporate Identity & Context Selection
- Employee Benefits Dashboard
- HRMS Integration
- Digital Health Card
- Claims & Coverage Tracking
- Dependent Management
- Secure Role-Based Access Control
